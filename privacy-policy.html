Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This privacy policy describes how this application ("the App") accesses, uses, and stores information in connection with the Instagram Graph API and Facebook Login for Business.

1. Who runs this App

This App is a personal, single-user automation tool built and operated by its owner solely to publish content (Reels and related posts) to the owner's own Instagram professional account. It is not a public-facing service, and no third-party user accounts are onboarded or managed by this App.

2. What information is accessed

The App accesses the following information via the Instagram Graph API and Facebook Graph API, limited strictly to the owner's own connected Instagram Business/Creator account and linked Facebook Page:

  • Instagram Business Account ID and basic account identifiers
  • Facebook Page ID and Page access token, used solely to resolve the linked Instagram account
  • Media publishing status (container creation and processing status) for content the owner uploads

3. How information is used

All accessed information is used exclusively to publish content (Reels/videos) that the owner has created, to the owner's own Instagram account. No data is used for advertising, profiling, analytics resale, or any purpose beyond enabling this publishing workflow.

4. Data storage and sharing

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored only on infrastructure controlled by the App owner (a private server) and are never transmitted to or shared with any third party, advertiser, or external service. No data collected via this App is sold, rented, or disclosed to outside parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Access tokens are retained only as long as needed for the App to function and are refreshed or revoked at the owner's discretion. The owner may revoke the App's access at any time via Instagram or Facebook account settings, which immediately invalidates all associated tokens.

6. Third-party services

This App interacts only with Meta's official Instagram Graph API and Facebook Graph API. It does not integrate with, or share data with, any other third-party platform.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or applicable requirements. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or the App's data practices, please contact the App owner directly through the account associated with this application.
